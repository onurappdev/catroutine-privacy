CatRoutine - Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 6, 2026

Overview

CatRoutine is a cat care routine tracker. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Data Collection

CatRoutine does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All data — including cat profiles, routines, and settings — is stored locally on your device.

Third-Party Services

CatRoutine does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Notifications

CatRoutine uses local notifications to remind you of upcoming routines. These notifications are scheduled entirely on your device and no data is sent to any server.

Photos

If you add a photo for your cat's profile, it is stored locally on your device only. It is never uploaded or shared.

Children's Privacy

CatRoutine does not knowingly collect any data from children or any other users.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
