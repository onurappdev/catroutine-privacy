CatRoutine - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Overview

CatRoutine is a cat care routine tracker. This policy describes CatRoutine 2.0, including local care records, subscriptions and usage analytics.

Data Collection

Cat profiles, routines, care history, health observations, weights, notes, the owner name and phone number you may enter, and photos are stored on your device. CatRoutine does not operate a cloud account or upload this content to our servers. Your device backup settings may include app data. Subscription and usage data are processed by the services described below.

Third-Party Services

CatRoutine uses TelemetryDeck to understand app usage, such as opening the app, creating or completing a routine, starting a health track, and viewing an offer. Signals may include the app version, operating system, device language, the language chosen in the app, your subscription state (for example trial or active), counts in fixed ranges (such as the number of cats or routines), whether notifications are allowed, how many widgets are placed, and technical usage information. Event parameters use limited categories; we do not send cat names, owner names, phone numbers, free-text notes, profile photos, observation photos or report contents to analytics.

We use RevenueCat and Apple to validate purchases and restore Premium access. This involves an app user identifier, purchase and subscription information, and technical information needed to process the transaction. If you installed the app from an Apple Search Ads advertisement, Apple AdServices attribution (such as the campaign, ad group and keyword) is collected through RevenueCat to measure which ads lead to installations and subscriptions. We do not receive your payment-card details.

Installation identifier

On first launch the app creates a random identifier for this installation. It is not derived from your name, email, Apple Account, phone number or device advertising identifier. TelemetryDeck receives it only in hashed form as an anonymous analytics user, and it is stored with RevenueCat so that anonymous usage, purchases and ad attribution of the same installation can be analysed together. We do not use it to identify you or to track you across other companies' apps or websites. Deleting and reinstalling the app creates a new identifier.

CatRoutine 2.0 does not display third-party advertising or request the advertising identifier (IDFA).

Notifications

CatRoutine uses local notifications to remind you of upcoming routines. Reminder content is scheduled on your device. Whether notification permission was granted may be included in a usage signal; reminder text is not sent with that signal.

Photos

Photos you select for a profile or health observation are stored locally. The app can create a veterinary PDF, care guide or backup file when you request one. You choose whether to include observation photos in a veterinary PDF and where to save or share a file. Files you export may contain names, notes and photos; the destination you choose handles its own copy. CatRoutine does not automatically send these files to a veterinarian or to an analytics service.

Children's Privacy

CatRoutine does not ask for a child’s name, age or contact information. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support request, please contact us.

Your choices

You can turn off notifications in the app or in iPhone Settings. Because analytics and purchase data are tied only to a random installation identifier, we generally cannot link them to you; if you contact us we will help with any request we are able to fulfil.

Deletion and contact

You can delete local records in the app and remove app data from your device. Copies you exported or included in device backups must be managed separately. Deleting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription. For questions about subscription or service data, contact us using the address below.

Changes
